Project overview

Connecting Ghanaian talent to global opportunities

A trusted gateway for candidates exploring international work and recruitment support.

Eso Jobs is a premium recruitment platform designed to bridge the gap between skilled Ghanaian professionals and verified international job opportunities. The responsive website presents the services clearly and is optimized for search and conversion.

The platform is live at esojobs.co and receives applications and candidate enquiries. It includes a job flyer gallery with an admin panel, video testimonials, and a floating WhatsApp channel for candidate support.

The problem / Our solution

From fragmented job hunting to a trusted gateway

Challenges faced

  • No trusted channel to verified international jobs for skilled Ghanaian professionals.
  • Manual application handling and scattered job flyers without a central gallery.
  • Low search visibility made it difficult for candidates to find the platform.
  • No structured way to manage and publish job opportunities.
  • Limited communication channels for candidate support and enquiries.
  • Inconsistent brand presentation across a growing recruitment service.

Our solutions

  • Trust-building design system with consistent navy and orange branding.
  • Interactive job flyer gallery managed through a password-protected admin panel.
  • Semantic structure and search optimization with a reported SEO score of 92/100.
  • Prominent application actions and a candidate application form.
  • WhatsApp contact, video testimonials and parallax interactions.
