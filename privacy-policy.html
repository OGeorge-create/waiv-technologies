Privacy Policy

How TechWaiv collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Home  /  Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as contact details submitted through our forms, as well as technical data such as browser type, device information, and usage patterns to improve the website.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies to provide essential site functionality, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors engage with our website. Some cookies are strictly necessary, while others are optional and only used if you consent.

3. Cookie categories

  • Necessary: required for security, accessibility, and basic site operations.
  • Analytics: helps us understand traffic and improve performance.
  • Preferences: remembers choices such as language or region.
  • Marketing: used for relevant campaigns and promotional content.

4. Your choices

You can accept all cookies, reject optional categories, or customize your preferences at any time using the cookie settings control on our website.

5. Third-party services

We may use trusted third-party services for analytics, hosting, or embedded content. These services may process some information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, please contact us through the contact page on this website.
