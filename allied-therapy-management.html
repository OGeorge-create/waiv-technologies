How We Built a Therapy Management System
for a Ghana Clinic

A comprehensive therapy management system with patient records, clinical assessments, SOAP notes and multi-role access for therapy centers in Ghana.

ClientTherapy center in Ghana
UsersAdmins, therapists, staff & receptionists
ScopeClinical & administrative workflows
PlatformCodeIgniter 3 / MySQL

The project

Bring clinical records and therapy workflows together

A secure workspace for patient progress, clinical documentation and the teams coordinating care.

When a therapy center in Ghana approached TechWaiv to digitize its operations, it needed more than basic patient management. The clinic required a comprehensive system for complex clinical workflows, multiple assessment types and secure documentation for therapy sessions.

We collaborated with therapists and clinic administrators to understand their workflows and documentation requirements. The resulting platform supports different therapy disciplines while organizing clinical and administrative work in one place.

Problem / Approach

Move from scattered paperwork to coordinated care

What made clinic operations difficult

  • Paper-based records made patient information difficult to coordinate.
  • Assessment tracking was inconsistent across therapy sessions.
  • Scheduling across multiple therapists required substantial manual effort.
  • Clinical notes could be incomplete, with no central view of progress or outcomes.

How we approached the system

  • Consulted therapists and clinic staff about their day-to-day workflows.
  • Researched therapy assessment methods and documentation standards.
  • Designed around clinical workflow efficiency and patient progress tracking.
  • Applied role-based access to protect sensitive patient information.

A connected platform for therapy centers

Clinical, scheduling and administrative modules work together to support the full rhythm of a therapy center.

Multi-role access

Role-specific access for administrators, therapists, staff and receptionists.

Patient management

Complete patient profiles with medical history and document storage.

Therapy scheduling

Coordinate therapist availability and track patient appointments.

Clinical documentation

Record therapy sessions in structured SOAP notes with file attachments.

Assessment frameworks

Support speech and language, occupational, cognitive and audiology evaluations.

Manage invoices and keep payment information connected to clinic workflows.

Reports and analytics

Review attendance, therapy plans and monthly performance information.

Department organization

Organize work across specialties in a multi-discipline therapy center.

Clinical / Administrative workflows

Support patient care and the work around it

Clinical features

  • Structured SOAP notes for subjective, objective, assessment and plan details.
  • Multiple assessment types with standardized scoring and progress tracking.
  • Patient attendance sign-in and sign-out with archival history.
  • Therapy plan creation and review workflows.
  • Department-based organization for multi-specialty clinics.

Administrative capabilities

  • Role-based dashboards tailored to each user type.
  • Notifications for appointments and system updates.
  • Expense tracking and financial reporting.
  • Database backup and system configuration tools.
  • Multi-language support for clinic staff.

Results / Value

More coordinated documentation and patient care

The implementation transformed clinic operations and patient care, with a reported 90% reduction in administrative paperwork through digital documentation.

Results for the therapy center

  • Improved clinical coordination through centralized patient records.
  • Enhanced assessment tracking and therapy outcome measurement.
  • Streamlined billing and payment processes.
  • Better staff productivity with role-specific dashboards.

Why digital therapy management matters

  • Comprehensive documentation supports informed patient care.
  • Structured workflows help clinics scale as demand grows.
  • Organized records and assessments support professional practice.
  • Reporting provides insight into therapy programs and outcomes.

Lessons and next steps

Build healthcare software around clinical practice

The project reinforced the importance of clinical workflow expertise, flexible assessment frameworks, staff training and careful data security in healthcare applications.

Future updates will focus on modern password hashing, enhanced mobile interfaces and integration with telemedicine platforms.
