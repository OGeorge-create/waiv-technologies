WAIV-IN Inventory Management System

WAIV-IN is a comprehensive web-based inventory management and retail operations system developed to simplify stock management, sales processing and business reporting.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Streamlining retail operations with intelligent inventory management

WAIV-IN was developed to address the complex inventory management needs of modern retail operations in Ghana. The system provides businesses with better visibility over stock, sales and daily operations.

The platform supports multi-location inventory tracking across warehouses and a central store, helping businesses monitor stock levels and process sales more efficiently.

Key Features

Inventory Tracking

Monitor stock levels across multiple locations with improved visibility and control.

Sales Checkout

Process sales efficiently while keeping transaction records organized.

Role-Based Access

Different users can access the functions appropriate to their responsibilities.

WhatsApp AI Integration

Admin users can interact with business information through WhatsApp-based queries.

Receipt Printing

Generate and print transaction receipts as part of the sales workflow.

Business Reports

Generate useful reports that help management understand business performance.

Technology & Implementation

WAIV-IN was built using CodeIgniter 3 and structured around the operational requirements of the business. The system combines inventory management, sales processing, reporting and user access management within one platform.
