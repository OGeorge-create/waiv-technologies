How We Built a Dental Management System
for a Ghana Clinic

A clinic website, appointment workflow and patient management system built for a dental practice in Ghana.

ClientClyda Dental
LocationKumasi, Ghana
ScopeWebsite & clinic system
WorkflowsBooking & patient care

The project

One connected digital experience for patients and staff

Bring online bookings, patient records and daily clinic workflows into one coordinated service.

When Clyda Dental asked TechWaiv to modernize clinic operations, they needed more than a website. They needed a digital system combining online bookings, patient records and clinic workflows in one place.

The solution brings together a patient-facing website and an internal dental management system. Local user needs, mobile-first access and straightforward workflows for receptionists and dental assistants shaped the experience.

Problem / Approach

Replace paper processes with a practical clinic workflow

What was getting in the way

  • Manual appointment logs and phone-based bookings created avoidable admin work.
  • Enquiries, double bookings and paperwork took staff time away from patients.
  • Patient information was spread across records and spreadsheets.
  • Staff needed a better way to coordinate bookings, billing and patient files.

How we approached the system

  • Grounded the design in local user research with clinic staff and patients.
  • Designed mobile-first for patients who primarily use smartphones.
  • Kept common tasks straightforward for receptionists and dental assistants.
  • Provided an admin dashboard for appointments, billing and patient files.

What we built

Two connected parts of the clinic experience

A clear public website helps patients find care, while the management system organizes the work behind each visit.

Clinical Module

Document dental examinations, treatment plans, diagnoses, and clinical notes on a per-patient basis.

Mobile-first access

Responsive pages and workflows for patients using phones, tablets and desktop devices.

Patient Management

Register, update, and manage complete patient profiles including medical history, treatment records, and contact information.

Appointments & Scheduling

Book, confirm, reschedule, or cancel appointments with live status tracking. Visual appointment overview for staff efficiency.

Billing & Invoicing

Generate invoices, record partial payments, and track outstanding balances. Full payment history per patient.

Inventory Management

Track dental supplies, consumables, and instruments across branches with low-stock alerts and stock value reporting.

Prescriptions

Create and manage medication prescriptions linked directly to patient records and treatment plans.

Reports & Analytics

Revenue trends, appointment analytics, patient activity, treatment summaries, and inventory reports, all filterable by date and branch.

Practice workflow

Connect bookings, patient care and operations

Patient-facing website

  • Explain dental services in patient-friendly language.
  • Keep pages fast-loading for lower-bandwidth connections.
  • Provide contact forms and an appointment request flow.
  • Use local search-friendly content and clinic location details.

Dental management system

  • Capture patient registration details and medical history.
  • Schedule appointments with dentist assignment and visit tracking.
  • Manage invoices, payments and dental inventory.
  • Generate daily reports and patient follow-up reminders.

Results / Local impact

A more organized digital experience for Clyda Dental

Results for the clinic

  • Faster appointment booking and fewer routine phone enquiries.
  • Less paperwork through structured patient records.
  • Clearer visibility for staff through a shared dashboard.
  • A polished website that supports trust with local patients.

Why this matters for Ghana businesses

  • Local user needs and practical workflows stay central to the product.
  • Digital records help clinics manage growing demand more consistently.
  • Online booking gives patients a direct route to clinic services.
  • Connected tools make day-to-day work more reliable for service teams.
