Project overview

A welcoming digital home for patient care

Make care easier to understand, build trust and give prospective patients a clear way to get in touch.

The Allied Therapy Clinic came to TechWaiv to create a website that reflects its compassionate, professional approach to therapy. The result is a modern, patient-focused website that clearly presents the clinic's services, team and contact information.

Fully deployed and live, the website is designed to build trust with prospective patients, make services easy to understand and provide clear pathways for contacting the clinic.

The problem / Our solution

From low visibility to a trusted local presence

Challenges faced

  • The clinic had no meaningful online presence to reach new patients.
  • Prospective patients could not easily learn about services or get in touch.
  • The clinic team and its expertise needed a clearer presentation.
  • Low search visibility made the clinic difficult to find locally.
  • The website needed to convey warmth and professional healthcare trust.

Our solutions

  • A clean design that reflects the clinic's caring brand.
  • Clear service pages that help patients understand what is offered.
  • A dedicated team section to build credibility and human connection.
  • Search-optimized structure and content for local visibility.
  • A responsive layout for patients on any device.
