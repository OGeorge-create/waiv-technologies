How We Built the Eso Jobs
Recruitment Management System

A secure, comprehensive recruitment and candidate management platform that supports Eso Jobs' operations from candidate registration and application tracking to appointments and invoicing.

ClientEso Jobs
UsersAdmins, recruiters, receptionists & candidates
ScopeRecruitment operations
PlatformCodeIgniter / MVC

The project

One system for the full recruitment operation

Connect candidate records, job orders, appointments and finance in a secure team workflow.

While the public Eso Jobs website connects candidates to opportunities, a dedicated platform manages the operation behind the scenes. TechWaiv built a recruitment and candidate management system for registering candidates, tracking applications, scheduling appointments and handling invoicing.

Recruitment involves candidates at different stages, job orders from client companies, appointments, payments and a team with distinct responsibilities. The platform brings these moving parts into a centralized workflow designed around the way the team works.

Problem / Approach

Make complex recruitment work easier to coordinate

What made operations difficult

  • Candidate records and applications move through multiple stages.
  • Job orders, appointments, payments and team activity need coordination.
  • Scattered processes increase manual effort and make information harder to secure.
  • Multiple branches and staff roles require carefully scoped access.

How the platform responds

  • Role-based dashboards give users access to the tools they need.
  • Branch-aware access control uses session state and helper libraries to scope records.
  • A clean login flow routes users to their role-specific workspace.
  • Secure candidate records and documents within a maintainable MVC system.

System capabilities

A connected recruitment platform

Interconnected modules support the people, opportunities and administrative work involved in recruitment.

Multi-role authentication

Distinct dashboards for admins, recruiters, receptionists and candidates.

Candidate management

Candidate registration, profiles, document management and ledgers.

Jobs and companies

Manage client companies and the job orders that drive recruitment activity.

Application pipeline

Track application stages through filterable views and coordinated workflows.

Appointment scheduling

Coordinate appointments with a calendar view and automated reminders.

Payments and invoicing

Handle invoices, payment tracking, receipts, expenses and reporting.

Web push notifications

Deliver real-time updates to recruiters as recruitment activity progresses.

Branch management

Configure branches and organization-wide settings for super admins.

Operational workflows

Follow candidates from registration through placement

Candidate lifecycle

  • Add, edit, update and view candidate records.
  • Review complete candidate profiles and histories.
  • Manage candidate documents with file and folder organization.
  • Keep records and candidate ledgers branch-scoped.
  • Search and paginate large candidate lists.

Pipeline, appointments and finance

  • Manage job orders and client company records.
  • Move and filter applications through pipeline stages.
  • Use AJAX calendar feeds and appointment status updates.
  • Create invoices, track payments and print receipts.
  • Record expenses and organize transaction categories.

Results / Value

A central workspace for the work behind every placement

The management system is live and actively powering Eso Jobs' recruitment operations.

Results for Eso Jobs

  • A secure, centralized place for recruitment data.
  • Role-based workflows aligned to how the team works.
  • Streamlined candidate, job and application-pipeline management.
  • Scheduling, payments and reporting in the same operation.
  • Branch-aware access and system configuration for administrators.

Why a dedicated system matters

  • Centralized candidate information supports faster, better-informed decisions.
  • Connected workflows reduce repetitive manual work and human error across the pipeline.
  • Role-based access helps protect sensitive information.
  • Recruiting, scheduling and finance can move through one coordinated process.
  • Reporting gives the team information to improve placement operations.

Lessons and next steps

Build around real workflows and strong access controls

The project reinforced the value of role-based design for complex operations, branch-aware access for multi-team data, and connecting candidate, pipeline and finance modules in a maintainable MVC system.

Future improvements will continue to enhance pipeline analytics, the candidate experience and reporting capabilities.
