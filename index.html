About

TechWaiv is the operating brand of Waiv Technologies., a Ghana-based technology company built to support ambitious businesses with reliable software and digital products — at home and internationally.

A Technology Partner Built to Scale.

TechWaiv is growing from a founder-led business into a professionally managed technology company built around documented processes, clear accountability and measurable delivery.
